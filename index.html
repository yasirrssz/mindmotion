MindMotion

Pick an imagined action, replay a real EEG recording, and see how brain signals become a message.

Simulate what the user imagines

Pipeline

  1. 1. RecordFour sensors on the head capture brain activity.
  2. 2. CleanFilters remove power-line hum, blinks and drift.
  3. 3. MeasureChecks how strong the brain’s resting rhythm is at each sensor.
  4. 4. DecideThe model picks the movement the person imagined.
  5. 5. ExplainTurns the result into a sentence and a command.

Signals are real EEG from the PhysioNet EEG Motor Movement/Imagery Dataset (Schalk et al., 2004). Each person has a small model trained on their own earlier trials; the recordings replayed here were held out from training. The sentence explanation is a template, not yet an LLM. Accuracy is modest with only four sensors.
